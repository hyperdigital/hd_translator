# Changelog 3.8.0

## The demand screen answers its question before it shows its evidence

The screen held eleven blocks, each answering its own question at the whole site, and left the
reader to join them up by hand. Somebody opening it wants one thing: *which language should we
build next, and is the number behind it real?* That answer was reachable but never stated.

- **The answer is now the first thing on the screen.** How many languages are worth building,
  which ones they are, and how close the rest are. The threshold the verdict rests on used to be
  ours; it is now a slider the reader moves, and moving it re-counts the answer and re-labels
  every language on the screen at once.

- **A language picked anywhere is the whole screen.** Click a language in the answer, in the
  legend, in the grid or in the table and the chart, the banner funnel and the pages all
  re-answer for that one language; the funnel in particular was site-wide and therefore said
  nothing about the language the reader was deciding on. `× all languages` puts the screen back.
  Holding alt on a legend key still only hides that series, the way it did before.

- **The chart draws five lines, not thirty-two.** The five that moved most in the last week, with
  everything else as one band beneath them, so what is left out is still visible as a quantity.
  `All` brings the rest back, and a log scale makes seventy a day and four a day readable in the
  same picture - which is the comparison the screen is for.

- **Every language at once, as a grid of cells.** One card each: the month, its own sparkline,
  the trend, and what to do about it in two words. Filters for the ones with no real pages, the
  ones growing, and the ones the banner reports on. This is what reading thirty-two lines out of
  one chart was standing in for.

- **The language inflating its own number is named.** The count of requests asking for the
  language the page already was has been on the screen since 3.6.0; which language it is was
  not, and it is the one thing needed to go and fix the banner.

- **The pages that carry the demand across all languages** are listed before the per-language
  lists, which answer a different question and were the only answer offered.

## Fixes

- The `hidden` attribute lost against Bootstrap's `.btn` rule, and would have lost against the
  new blocks' own display rules: controls and cards could appear before they had anything to say.
