# Changelog 2.2.8

Stays in the 2.2.x line: 2.3.0 through 2.3.2 are taken by the other branch of this same
TYPO3 13 line, so this one cannot have them even though the change is a feature.

## New Features
- The demand screen answers four more questions, three of them without storing anything that
  was not already there:

  - **Which days the asking happens on**, read out of the day each counting row already carries.
  - **Whether the demand is concentrated or spread** - the share held by the busiest three
    languages and the sum of the squared shares as an index. That is the difference between
    building one mutation and building several.
  - **What each language has cost**, in characters. DeepL charges by the character and the
    source texts are already in the cache, so this is the closest thing to a bill the
    installation can answer without writing a number anywhere.
  - **Where the asking happens** - the pages a translation was requested on, busiest first.

- Months outlive their days. When the retention drops the daily rows, the prune first folds them
  into one row per language per month, so a decade of history costs less than a day of detail.
  It adds rather than replaces, which keeps it correct when the retention boundary falls inside
  a month, and running twice changes nothing because the rows are gone by then.

  What a month holds is **visitor-days**, not people: somebody who asked on three days counts
  three times. The distinct people of a month cannot be recovered once the daily rows are gone,
  because the visitor hash is re-salted every day on purpose, and the column does not pretend
  otherwise.

## Privacy
- The page a request came from is taken from the referer the browser already sends, so nothing
  changes in the frontend or in a project using it. Only the path is kept, never the query, so
  what a visitor typed into a search or a form never reaches the table. It counts requests, not
  people - a page is not a visitor, and pairing the two would multiply the rows by the size of
  the site.

## Changes
- The page table is bounded on both ends by construction: a language-day accepts at most fifty
  distinct pages, a day that has closed keeps the busiest ten, and pages are dropped after
  ninety days even where the counting rows live a year.

## Fixes
- The chart asked for `--typo3-text-color` and `--typo3-border-color`, which TYPO3 13 and 14 do
  not set, so both fell back to the light-mode colours written beside them: on a dark backend
  the legend labels came out near-black on near-black, about 1.02:1.
- The trend colours could not serve both backend themes from one hex - the rising green read
  2.64:1 on the light surface, the falling red 3.56:1 on the dark one. The surfaces differ
  between majors as well, TYPO3 14 being darker in light mode and lighter in dark mode, so each
  value was measured on both. The four now clear 4.5:1 on all four surfaces.

## Upgrade
- Run the database analyzer. Two tables are added, `tx_hdtranslator_ai_request_month` and
  `tx_hdtranslator_ai_request_page`; both start empty and fill as requests come in.
