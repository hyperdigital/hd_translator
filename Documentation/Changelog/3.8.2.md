# Changelog 3.8.2

## Fixes

- **The band of the remaining languages was drawn outside the chart and swallowed the clicks of
  everything above it.** The five lines set the scale and the band was drawn on it afterwards,
  so a band taller than the tallest line ran off the top of the plot - on the installation this
  was found on, nine hundred pixels past it, as a grey shape lying over the toolbar, the answer
  card and its language chips. None of those could be clicked. Three things were wrong and all
  three are fixed:

  - The band is now part of the scale it is drawn on. Whatever is in the picture sets the
    height of the picture.
  - `Top 5` ranks the five **and** the band on the range the chart draws, not on the
    last seven days. A language that was huge three weeks ago and quiet since used to miss the
    five and take its spike into the band, where it was five times the height of every line
    above it. The five and the rest are one picture and are now ranked on one window.
  - The chart clips to its own figure. It carried `overflow: visible` since the day it was
    written, which is what let a shape leave the plot and reach the rest of the screen at all.
    The band also stops taking pointer events: it is a backdrop, and the readout and the drag
    belong to the plot under it.
