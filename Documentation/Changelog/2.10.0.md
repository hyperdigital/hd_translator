# Changelog 2.10.0

## New Features
- **`How it got them`: the daily chart can say where a language's visitors came from.** The
  demand line does not move - it gains a boundary inside it and a second half below the axis:

  - filled inside the line, the visitors the **banner** brought;
  - the rest of the line, everybody who asked another way - a language switcher, a remembered
    choice, a direct link, or somebody who ignored the banner and translated anyway;
  - below the axis, the people the banner was **shown to who never asked** for a translation.

  The part inside the line can never overflow it, because it is a part of it: the demand rows and
  the banner rows carry the same day-salted visitor hash for the same person, so this is a set
  operation over one key rather than two aggregate numbers being compared. How many people were
  offered a translation says nothing about how many asked for one - either can be the larger -
  which is why those two live on opposite sides of the axis and only their overlap is drawn
  inside the line.

- Both halves share one scale, so their heights compare directly, and the chart grows taller
  while the split is on so neither half is squeezed flat.

## Changes
- A day the banner reported nothing about is drawn without a split, and the readout says so. A
  day with no banner rows is not a day on which the banner brought nobody, and drawing the two
  alike would turn "nobody measured" into "the banner is useless".
- The split talks about one language at a time. Pointing at a language switches to it; switching
  the view on without pointing at anything picks the language with the most to say.
- It is offered in the `Lines` mode only - in the stacked modes the fill already means something
  else, and the button says so by going quiet.

## Upgrade
- No database change. Clear the caches so the new backend asset is served.
- The split needs the banner to be reporting - see the two `hdtranslator_recordBanner…` helpers.
  Without them the chart is exactly what it was.
